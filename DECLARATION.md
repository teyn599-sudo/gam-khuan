## Declaration / 聲明內文

I am an ordinary person who writes code, with a mind that likes to think about the future of the Earth.

我是一個寫程式碼的普通人，空有一個喜歡思考地球未來的頭腦。

I know that sea-level rise and the global energy crisis are challenges faced by all of humanity. This "Gam-Khuan Planetary Geological Solid-State Energy System" is the system architecture blueprint I have devoted my heart to designing. I do not have huge funds to build it, nor do I intend to found a massive engineering corporation to monopolize it.

我深知海平面上升與全球能源危機是全人類共同面臨的考驗。這套「Gam-Khuan 地球工程與固態能源系統」是我傾盡心血設計出的系統架構藍圖。我沒有巨額資金去建造它，也無意成立龐大的工程企業去壟斷它。

Therefore, I hereby officially declare to the world:

因此，我在此向全世界正式宣布：

### 1. Complete Open Use / 完全開放使用

Any government, research institution, corporation, foundation, or individual worldwide — as long as you have the relevant technology and resources — may freely and without charge use, develop, experiment with, build, and commercialize it.

全球任何政府、研究機構、企業、基金會或個人，只要你具備相應的技術與資源，皆可自由無償拿去使用、開發、實驗、建造與商業化。

**I permanently waive any expensive patent royalties and operational revenue shares.**

**我永久放棄任何昂貴的專利許可費（Royalty）與營運抽成。**

### 2. Voluntary Appreciation Gift / 隨喜紅包

If any entity successfully initiates this architecture, receives funding, or officially begins construction, and if you are willing, please give me a voluntary appreciation gift.

若有任何單位成功將此架構立項、獲得資助或正式動工，若你們願意，請給我包一個隨喜紅包即可。

The amount is unlimited. It represents respect for knowledge and a blessing of inheritance, allowing me to eat well and continue living.

金額不限，代表對知識的尊重與一份傳承的祝福，讓我能安心吃飽飯、繼續生活。

### 3. Attribution / 留名於世

If you believe my ideas are indeed feasible and contribute even a little to this world, please preserve my name — **Hung, Ta-Piao (洪大標)** — in public documents, memorial plaques, or technical literature related to the project.

如果你們覺得我的想法確實可行、對這個世界有一點點貢獻，請在計畫相關的公開文件、紀念牌或技術文獻中，保留我的名字——**洪大標（Hung, Ta-Piao）**。

Let future generations know that there was once a Taiwanese code compiler who left such a thought and blessing for this Earth.

讓後人知道，曾有這麼一個台灣的程式碼編譯者，為這個地球留下過這樣一份思考與祝福。
